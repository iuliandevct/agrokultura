Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 | Effective Date: November 8, 2026

1. Introduction

Agrokultura ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, use our agricultural management software, or connect through our authorized API integrations (including Meta Platforms and TikTok for Developers).

2. Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following categories of data:

  • Account & Contact Data: Name, email address, phone number, and business details provided during registration or inquiry.
  • Agricultural & Farm Operation Data: Equipment preferences, crop listings, and analytics parameters you choose to manage within the platform.
  • Connected Platform Data (Meta / TikTok APIs): When you explicitly authorize our application via OAuth, we receive authorized identifiers (e.g. Ad Account ID, Page ID, or TikTok User ID) and temporary access tokens required to perform your requested actions (such as campaign reporting, MCP interactions, or video publishing). We do not store user passwords.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Agrokultura platform and tools.
  • Authenticate authorized requests and interface with Meta Ads MCP and TikTok Publishing endpoints.
  • Deliver reporting insights and campaign metrics requested by you.
  • Ensure security, debug issues, and comply with legal requirements and platform policies.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Data is only shared with authorized third-party service providers (such as Meta Platforms Inc., ByteDance / TikTok, and cloud hosting providers) solely to the extent necessary to fulfill your requested integrations.

5. User Data Deletion Instructions (Meta & TikTok Compliance)

In compliance with Meta Platform Terms, TikTok Developer Terms, and GDPR regulations, you have the absolute right to request the complete deletion of all data associated with your account or third-party connections.

How to request data deletion:

  1. Send an email to contact@agrokultura.ro with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".
  2. Include your account email address or your Meta App Scoped User ID / TikTok username.
  3. Alternatively, you can remove the Agrokultura app from your Facebook Settings > Apps and Websites or TikTok Settings > Security > Manage App Permissions, which immediately revokes our API access tokens.

Upon receiving your request, our team will purge all stored tokens, user identifiers, and associated cached records within 48 hours and send you written confirmation.

6. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard encryption (HTTPS / TLS) for all data in transit. API tokens are stored securely with restricted access and deleted automatically upon token expiration or user disconnection.
